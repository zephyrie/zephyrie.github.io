# zephyrie.com — Design Context

## Users

Hiring/recruiting contacts (CTOs, VPs of Eng, exec recruiters), healthcare-AI peers and academics, and the developer-relations community. Most arrive cold via a LinkedIn link or a conference bio. They spend 60–120 seconds, scanning for **(a)** "is this person credible at the level I need?" and **(b)** "do they think the way I want my team to think?" The site has to answer both inside the first viewport.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **considered, technical, evangelical**. Senior — Michael runs platform strategy and a 4.5M-download community at NVIDIA, not a junior dev. Should read like the personal site of someone who has opinions and ships them, not a templated portfolio. Warmth over corporate polish; precision over decoration.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Shipped variant: Brutalist** — Antonio condensed display + Geist Mono body, hard 1px borders, no rounding, semantic spacing scale (`--s-1`…`--s-9`).

**Palette: Cobalt** — cobalt blue + pewter gray on cool-tinted neutrals.
- **Cobalt** (`oklch(72% 0.16 250)` dark / `oklch(45% 0.20 255)` light) = identity & achievement: section numbers, org names, metric callouts, bullet markers, product-name highlights.
- **Pewter** (`oklch(70% 0.04 240)` dark / `oklch(48% 0.04 240)` light) = metadata & navigation: dates, sub-tags (with left-border accent), links, scope underline, focus rings, skip link.
- **Neutrals** subtly cool-tinted (chroma 0.006–0.014 toward hue 240) for cohesion. Dark mode is deliberately *not* max-contrast: off-white text `oklch(88%)` on lifted charcoal `oklch(17%)` (~13.3:1, well above AAA) to avoid halation/bloom. Borders ride the same softened off-white.

Constraints:
- **No glowing neon / cyberpunk.** No cyan-on-black, no purple→blue gradients.
- **No card grid of identical icon-title-sentence boxes.**
- **No literal 8-bit / pixel art.**
- Both light and dark via `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Antonio + Geist Mono only. Neither is in the AI reflex list.

## Design Principles

1. **One bold conceptual move per variant, then restraint everywhere else.** Don't layer effects.
2. **Typography is the design.** Type pairing + scale + rhythm carries each variant; chrome (cards, shadows, gradients) is suspect by default.
3. **Content is senior, so the layout must be too.** Generous space, clear hierarchy, no over-explained sections.
4. **Numbers earn their size.** 4.5M downloads, 171K views, 6K developers — these are the receipts. Show them like receipts.
5. **The hero must answer "who, where, why-care" before the fold.** Everything else is supporting evidence.
